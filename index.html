Best in Spring

Coorg , Karnataka

Unwind in the lap of nature in Coorg where aromatic coffee and spice plantations, lush green hills, cascading waterfalls and a distinct old-world charm sooth the senses.

Best in Summer

Spiti , Himachal Pradesh

Looking for a break from city life? Say hello to Spiti! With pristine views, lush meadows, pine forests, and snow-capped mountains, it is the ideal escape to relax and rejuvenate. Oh! And the age-old monasteries make for a soulful visit that stays with you long after the trip is over.

Best in Monsoon

Cherapunji , Meghalaya

Known all over for experiencing the highest rainfall in the country, the landscape of Cherrapunji flashes brightly during the monsoon season.

Best in Autumn

Darjeeling , West Bengal

Post-monsoon skies clear up completely, offering crisp mountain air and unobstructed, panoramic views of the Kanchenjunga range.

Best in Winter

Gulmarg, Jammu & Kashmir

Ride one of the world's highest cable cars, ski powdery Himalayan slopes, and enjoy fresh snowfall across the meadows of this winter wonderland.

Best in Summer

Ladakh, India

Road-trip over high mountain passes, camp beside the shimmering Pangong Lake, and visit ancient Buddhist monasteries in the cold desert.
